Study planning tips

📘 Break big topics into smaller tasks

Schedule individual topics instead of trying to study an entire course at once.

⏱️ Use focused study blocks

Try 25–50 minutes of focused work followed by a short break.

🔁 Revise regularly

Revisit difficult topics over several days and test yourself without looking at your notes.

🌙 Protect your rest

Allow time for sleep, meals, lectures and other responsibilities.
